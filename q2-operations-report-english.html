23 — Root Cause Analysis · Fishbone 6M

Root cause: Manpower → Measurement

1 · MANPOWER

  • Low food-safety awareness — not everyone attended FHT
  • Weak supervision & monitoring — CCTV mis-placement
  • Low accountability — crew cultural background, review hiring criteria

2 · METHOD

  • No final inspection before serving — old culture prioritised speed
  • Weak pest management programme — pest-entry inspection not yet thorough (inspector: Aiman Haiqal)

3 · MACHINE

  • Insufficient chiller / freezer — thawing at room temperature (risky)
  • Insufficient dry storage — raw material stored off-premises (Cheras, KLTS, Setiawangsa, Wangsa Melawati)

4 · MATERIAL

  • Raw material from TGCK doesn't meet food-safety standards
  • Supplier quality remains the main driver of physical & biological complaints downstream

5 · ENVIRONMENT

  • Doors / windows left open — high humidity forces crew to open for ventilation, exposing premises to pests/rodents
  • Food warmers unused — ectothermic flies; outlets must use food warmers 100% (60°C)

6 · MEASUREMENT

  • No Pest / Rodent Matrix yet — matrix development underway at HQD
  • Wrong packaging box size — HQD flagged unsuitable size, project stalled after Adilah moved to CXCI
Conclusion: Staff background is the variable; the absence of a firm enforcement system is the constant.
